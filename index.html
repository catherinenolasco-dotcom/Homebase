People Operations built from zero

Ridecell | Dec 2018 - Jan 2026

Challenge

Ridecell had no documentation beyond outdated files, headcount lived in spreadsheets, and employees had no tracked channel for questions.

What I did

Built a 20+ procedure SOP library with owners and backup coverage, created ASKPeople (Jira and Confluence ticketing with tiered SLAs), and made Sapling the HR single source of truth, integrated with ADP and Zapier connections.

Result

Growth from 3 to 5 countries, 6 consecutive clean SOC 2 Type 2 audits, approval cycles cut from 1-2 days to 2-3 hours, and 5-10 tickets a day resolved in 24-48 hours.

What I would do differently

I'd have started the SOP library and ticketing system in the first months. Documenting as I went would have been easier than catching up later.

One approval path for every HRIS change

Prezent AI | Feb 2026 - Apr 2026

Challenge

HRIS changes ran through an undocumented, unapproved process, which created audit risk. Some people pushed back that a form would be too process-heavy.

What I did

Built a Claude-powered Employee Action Form (EAF), the payroll action form equivalent, requiring documentation and approval before any HRIS change, plus Notion New Hire and Offboarding workflows.

Result

100% adoption within one to two months, an audit trail that had not existed, and preboarding within 24 hours of offer acceptance.

What I would do differently

I'd have piloted the form with one team first. Some people said it was too process-heavy, and early proof from a small group might have won them over faster.

  • Claude
  • Notion
  • Rippling

A reorganization with cost visibility

Prezent AI | Feb 2026 - Apr 2026

Challenge

A reorganization affected 18 employees across the US and India, and leaders had no clear view of what it would cost.

What I did

Built AI-enabled severance and cost modeling (Claude and Gemini) that calculated severance, COBRA cost, and COBRA months owed and drafted agreements, tracked on a shared spreadsheet for Payroll and IT.

Result

First-time RIF cost visibility for leaders, and agreement prep cut from 20 minutes per employee to about 3 hours total.

What I would do differently

I'd have built the cost model before the reorganization was decided. Leaders could then have compared scenarios rather than just seeing the cost of the chosen one.

30-60-90

A first 90 days

A proposed plan built around the responsibilities in the role description.

Days 1 to 30

Listen and map

  • Meet the COO, Executive Director, leaders, and the external HR provider, and agree who owns what.
  • Map the handbook, policies, HRIS, payroll, and benefits calendar.
  • Read what employees and managers are already telling you.
Days 31 to 60

Design the first moves

  • Run a People Operations check across policies, records, and compliance with the provider.
  • Pick two or three quick fixes, such as one approval path for HR changes, the pattern behind the Employee Action Form.
  • Draft a manager feedback and expectations guide.
Days 61 to 90

Ship and measure

  • Pilot changes with one team before rolling them out.
  • Set a monthly rhythm for people trends and risks with the COO.
  • Start a simple recognition and engagement rhythm for a remote team.
By month 6

The outcome

  • Clear roles among HR, managers, leaders, and partners.
  • A documented employee lifecycle and a compliance calendar.
  • Managers who know how to coach, give feedback, and escalate.

This is a starting point. I would adjust it after the first conversations with the COO, the Executive Director, and the leadership team.
